# Legal Notice and Responsible Use

## General purpose

Gökdoğan Intelligence is developed for open-source information collection, visualization and situational awareness. It is not intended to provide unauthorized access to third-party systems or bypass access controls.

## User responsibility

Users are responsible for complying with:

- applicable law in their jurisdiction and in jurisdictions where data is collected/published,
- third-party provider terms of service,
- copyright, database-right and attribution requirements,
- privacy and personal-data protection obligations.

## Prohibited / unsupported examples

- unauthorized access to private or closed cameras,
- bypassing passwords or authentication,
- covert person-targeted surveillance, harassment or stalking,
- real-time tracking intended to evade law enforcement,
- using public military/strategic data for operational targeting or harm,
- bypassing third-party API quotas or access restrictions,
- redistributing provider data in violation of its license.

## Military and strategic data

Military-aircraft, infrastructure or base/reference information visible in the application may only come from public data sets. These views are not designed for:

- discovering secret facilities,
- obtaining closed telemetry,
- real-time targeting,
- operational attack planning.

## Camera data

The GitHub repository should not republish live third-party camera frames. Gökdoğan should open only public/authorized source URLs or display imagery through APIs where the provider permits it.

## Data accuracy

Information displayed in Gökdoğan may be:

- delayed,
- incomplete,
- misclassified,
- temporarily offline,
- cached.

It must not be used as the sole source for emergency, navigation, safety, health, investment or official decisions.

## Financial notice

Market data is informational and does not constitute investment advice.

The software is provided under AGPL-3.0. No guarantee is made that the application or third-party data will be continuously available, accurate or fit for a particular purpose.

## Reporting security vulnerabilities

Do not publish API keys or sensitive exploitation details in a public issue. Follow the project's [`../../SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md) policy.
