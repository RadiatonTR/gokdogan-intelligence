# Code of Conduct

Respectful and safe communication matters as much as technical quality in the Gökdoğan Intelligence community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people and differing viewpoints with respect.
- Direct criticism at technical ideas, not individuals.
- Provide reproducible information in bug reports whenever possible.
- Do not disclose security- or privacy-sensitive details in public channels.
- Respect licenses, terms of use and privacy boundaries for new data sources.
- Do not encourage illegal access, unauthorized data collection or harmful use.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, hate speech or personal attacks,
- doxxing or unauthorized disclosure of personal information,
- publishing API keys, passwords, tokens or other secrets,
- requesting or facilitating unauthorized access to private systems,
- content that facilitates operational tracking of sensitive persons, law-enforcement targets or military targets,
- spam or unrelated advertising.

## Enforcement

Content that violates these standards may be removed. Participants may be warned, temporarily restricted or permanently removed from project communication spaces depending on severity and repetition.

Use `SECURITY.en.md` for vulnerability-reporting guidance. General bugs and feature requests should use GitHub Issues.
